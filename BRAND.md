## 1. Brand Essence & Narrative

Ammy’s Coffee is an expanding specialty café brand born in Delhi NCR. It occupies the sweet spot between artisanal specialty coffee craft (100% premium Arabica, Cortados, Cold Brews, Matcha) and an inviting, high-energy community third-place (fluffy pancakes, handcrafted croissants, trending desserts, and comfortable seating).

### The Brand Voice
- **Tone:** Witty, conversational, warm, welcoming, youthful, and design-forward.
- **Personality:** The friend who knows exceptional coffee without being pretentious.
- **Audience:** Gen Z, Millennials, creative professionals, students, and young entrepreneurs across prime NCR neighborhoods (GK-1, Galleria Gurugram, SDA Market, Punjabi Bagh).

### Verified Brand Copy & Hooks (Extracted from Brand Deck)
- *"From that first pour of 100% Arabica to the last bite of our Tiramisu, everything here is made to feel like a warm hug in a cup."*
- *"This isn’t just your daily caffeine fix. It’s your slow morning ritual, your mid-day pick-me-up, your 'let’s catch up over coffee' kind of place."*
- *"Come for the coffee. Stay for the connection."*
- *"The Chill Spot You've Been Manifesting — Aesthetic. Cozy. Built for Vibes. Step into Ammy's and enter your second home where the lighting's soft, the art's bold, and the booths practically beg you to stay longer."*
- *"The Cravings We're Known For — From classic croissants to drama-filled espresso and frappes, our menu is here for your main character cravings."*
- *"Club Rewards: Slurp. Earn. Repeat. It's that simple."*

## 2. Logo System & Symbolism

The brand logo is composed of three harmonious elements:
1. **The Emblem (Crest):** Two curving coffee-leaf / bean wings forming a warm symmetrical crest. Symbolizes growth, comfort, craft, and the core motif: *"Two beans = one bond."*
2. **The Wordmark:** Friendly, approachable, modern lowercase `Ammy's` lettering with soft rounded terminals.
3. **The Subline:** Clean, tracked uppercase `— C O F F E E —` anchoring the mark in coffee authority.

### Logo Variations & Usage
- **White Mark (`ammy_logo_white.png`):** Primary logo for all dark surfaces, cinematic video overlays, and night-mode headers.
- **Dark Mark (`ammy_logo_dark.png`):** Secondary logo for light alabaster/cream backgrounds, receipts, and print.
- **Clear Space:** Maintain clear space equal to the height of the letter "A" on all sides. Never distort, recolor in neon, or drop drop-shadows on the mark.

## 4. Typography System

The typography creates a balance between **editorial sophistication** and **warm, approachable friendliness**:

### Display / Primary Headings:
- **Brand Font Reference:** `Omalt Regular`
- **Web Pairing:** *Playfair Display* / *Cormorant Garamond* / *Instrument Serif*
- **Role:** High-impact Hero headlines, section titles, editorial pull quotes (*"Two beans = one bond"*).
- **Styling:** Medium/Bold weights, tight letter spacing (`tracking-tight`), leading `1.1–1.2`.

### Body & Subtitles:
- **Brand Font Reference:** `Museo Sans Rounded` (300, 700, 900)
- **Web Pairing:** *Plus Jakarta Sans* / *Outfit* / *Inter*
- **Role:** Navigation links, body copy, menu item names, descriptions, buttons, forms.
- **Styling:** Clean geometric sans with soft curves, excellent readability on retina and mobile displays.

### Script / Accent:
- **Brand Font Reference:** `Gluck Regular`
- **Role:** Delicate sub-headers, editorial annotations, taglines, and category badges.

## 5. Imagery & Media Guidelines

### Café Architecture & Interior Photography:
- **Tone:** Soft ambient pendant lighting, natural wood tones, plush booth seating, bold café wall art.
- **Standard:** Use the 20 raw HDR/DSLR shots in `brand_materials/Ammy_s Folder/Interior Photos/`.
- **Avoid:** Over-saturated filters, artificial studio lighting, sterile corporate seating, or generic stock photography.

### Beverage & Food Photography:
- **Tone:** Real, appetizing, close-up textures—creamy espresso crema, glistening caramel drizzle on waffles, flaky croissant layers, vibrant green matcha.
- **Standard:** Extracted directly from their official menu assets and 4K commercial reels (`Ammy_s Store and Products 1-6.mp4`).

## 6. Brand Differentiators & Rules

| Do's | Don'ts |
| :--- | :--- |
| Celebrate 100% Arabica craft and artisanal kitchen. | Do not use cheap cliché vectors (steaming mugs, clip-art beans). |
| Highlight prime NCR locations (GK-1, Galleria Gurugram, SDA). | Do not make the brand feel like a budget generic quick-service diner. |
| Speak like a witty, design-conscious friend. | Do not sound corporate, dry, or stuffy. |
| Position the franchise as a proven, scalable business machine. | Never fabricate ROI figures, profit percentages, or misleading fees. |
