## 2. Brand Positioning & Dual-Audience Architecture

Ammy's Coffee is an expanding café brand with two distinct target audiences:

### Audience A: Retail Café Customers
- **Goal:** Brand discovery, appetite appeal, café exploration, and visiting physical stores.
- **Key Needs:**
  - Understand the brand ethos and café vibe.
  - Explore beverage, signature brew, and food offerings.
  - Find café locations, operational hours, and contact details.
  - Experience an appetizing, immersive, editorial online presence.

### Audience B: Prospective Franchise Partners (Crucial Commercial Priority)
- **Goal:** Partner acquisition and high-intent lead capture.
- **Key Needs:**
  - Dedicated "Franchise With Us" gateway (not an afterthought or hidden footer link).
  - Clear brand value proposition and expansion vision.
  - Transparent, frictionless lead submission form capturing:
    - Full Name, Email, Phone Number, Preferred City / Territory, Investment Capacity Bracket, Business / F&B Experience, and Additional Message.
  - **Absolute Rule:** Never fabricate franchise fees, profit margins, ROI promises, or financial claims.

## 3. Reference Websites & Competitive Benchmarks

### 1. The Waffle Co. (`https://thewaffleco.in/`)
- **Relationship:** Associated with the brand's business network; referenced for structural IA and franchise lead capture.
- **Guardrail:** Structural benchmark only; do NOT clone its visual design.

### 2. Kofi Kafi (`https://www.kofikafi.com/`)
- **Inspiration Takeaways:**
  - **Youthful Energy & Cultural Pulse:** Punchy, unapologetic Gen-Z copy ("We serve vibe", community movement, hustle culture).
  - **Smart Frictionless Actions:** Clean CTA buttons: `Find A Store` (Google Maps integration), `View Menu` (direct PDF access), and optional external `Order on Zomato / Swiggy` links.
  - **App-Free Ordering Strategy:** Proves that modern Indian café websites thrive without building complex custom ordering/cart systems—external routing to Swiggy/Zomato keeps the site fast, clean, and focused on brand discovery and franchise leads.
- **Ammy's Differentiation:** While Kofi Kafi is street-first / kiosk grab-and-go, Ammy's Coffee is an **upscale sit-down lifestyle café** (GK-1, Galleria Gurugram, SDA) with 100% Arabica, artisanal kitchen dishes, and higher-ticket franchise positioning.

## 5. Design Direction & Aesthetic Standards

### Visual Language
- **Vibe:** Premium, modern, editorial, warm, welcoming, youthful without being childish.
- **Photography:** High-contrast, rich, authentic coffee and café imagery; generous scale.
- **Typography:** Strong, elegant hierarchy (editorial display headings paired with clean, highly readable modern body type).
- **Layout & Spacing:** Intentional whitespace, editorial grid systems, asymmetric accents where appropriate, avoid cramped cards.
- **Motion & Interactions:** Restrained, smooth, micro-interactions, subtle reveals; avoid distracting or gimmicky animations.

### What to Strictly Avoid
- Generic "café bootstrap template" aesthetics.
- Monotonous walls of dark muddy brown.
- Cheesy coffee beans / steam vector clipart.
- Cluttered layouts or overused glassmorphism / neon gradients.
- Generic, ungrounded stock photography that misrepresents the real café experience.

## 6. Grounding & Truth Rules (Strict Hallucination Prevention)

1. **Brand Identity:** Use actual brand assets (logos, exact color hexes, typography, official imagery) as supplied.
2. **Menu & Offerings:** Do not invent fake items, pricing, or proprietary names unless explicitly approved as placeholders.
3. **Franchise & Financials:** Never invent franchise costs, royalty percentages, payback periods, or ROI figures.
4. **Unknowns Protocol:** When information is missing, document it explicitly as an **unconfirmed requirement / pending asset** rather than guessing.
